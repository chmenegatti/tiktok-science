3. Description of Service

The Application performs the following on behalf of its operator:

  • Generates a daily science topic and script.
  • Synthesizes narration and images.
  • Assembles a vertical video.
  • Optionally uploads the resulting video to the operator's TikTok inbox as a draft via the TikTok Content Posting API. The Application does not auto-publish content; all posting is performed manually by the operator.
